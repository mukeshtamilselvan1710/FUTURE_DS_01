## Task 1: Business Sales Dashboard Using E-commerce Data

Task Overview

In this task, I analyzed real-world e-commerce sales data to design an interactive **Business Sales Dashboard** using **Power BI** and **Excel**. The goal was to enable business owners and stakeholders to quickly evaluate sales performance and make informed, data-driven decisions.

The dashboard addresses key business questions, including:

* Which products are the top sellers?
* During which months do sales reach their peak?
* Which product categories and regions contribute the most to revenue?

This task did not involve coding; instead, it focused on **data analytics, visualization, and deriving business insights**.

---

 Work Performed

* Cleaned and organized raw sales data from Excel/CSV files
* Analyzed monthly sales trends, category-level performance, and region-wise revenue
* Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard with dynamic filters and visualizations
* Extracted insights and presented actionable business recommendations

---
Skills Developed

* Data cleaning and transformation
* Time-series and trend analysis
* Creating KPIs using DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* Business storytelling through data visualization
* Importing and managing Excel/CSV datasets in Power BI
